If someone asks you something at the end

“Why isn't there a subscription plan?”Because nobody has told me they'd pay for one. The evidence cuts both ways: Stylebook has lived seventeen years on a one-time price; Style DNA makes about three million dollars a year on subscriptions. So it's a scenario in the simulator, not a price on the page.

“Why is there no AI on this page?”A number that changes every time you ask it is useless for pricing, and it would cost money per question. The arithmetic is one file, and it has its own tests.

“What if someone fakes the numbers in the browser?”The server does the math again before saving. A test proves it: with zero registrations, the saved income is zero no matter what the browser sends.

“What was the privacy bug?”Week one's table let anyone read the text people wrote about their style using the public key. I checked, confirmed it, closed it in the database, and added it to the privacy notice. The new table stores a hash of the IP address, never the address.
